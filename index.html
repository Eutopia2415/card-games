    Your house rules

    Three players, 32 cards (7–A). Deal ten each and set two aside. Play clockwise. Round one starts with the 7♥ holder.

    Play matching ranks: singles, pairs or triples. Beat the same size with a higher rank. Four of a kind is an announced bomb that clears the table. A legally played ace also clears it; its player leads again.

    King (called mayor in the saved rules), Villager and Peasant are assigned by finishing order. Local practice points: 2, 1, 0.

    Later rounds: the King asks the Peasant three rank questions. A miss uses a question. A hit truthfully transfers one card of that rank; the King then returns one card per hit. The Villager draws the two set-aside cards, then chooses any two of the twelve cards to discard, exposed to everyone. The Peasant starts.

    Documented conventions

    The notes do not cover these edges: when 7♥ is set aside, the holder of the lowest dealt card starts. You may open with any legal set. Passing does not lock you out: all other active players must pass after the last play to clear the pile. If a player finishes on a clear or wins a pile after finishing, the next active player clockwise leads. No extra exchanges, revolution or Liar rules.

    Bots use only their own hand, public cards, hand sizes and exchange information they personally know. Their sampled estimates are approximate. There are no live move suggestions.
